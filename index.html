Oopbuy shopping guide

A good find deserves
a second look.

An Oopbuy finds list can help organize ideas. A useful shortlist goes further: it records what you know, what you still need to verify and what the purchase could cost.

  1. Choose a category and a purpose

    Start with the item you need: everyday shoes, a layering hoodie, a jersey or an accessory. Narrow by fit, material and intended use.

  2. Read the actual destination listing

    Confirm the selected variant, size chart, seller information and availability. A spreadsheet description may be incomplete or out of date.

  3. Compare evidence and total cost

    Review relevant photos and measurements, then include delivery, fees and applicable import charges in your budget.

  4. Keep notes before committing

    Record the product link, selected size, date checked and unanswered questions. Confirm return conditions before payment.

MeasurementsRelevant garment, foot or accessory measurements; units and measurement method.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy of each listing, so verify the destination page, product details and current availability.

Does this page contain a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and shopping guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, where you can explore the linked categories.

Is CAFANS the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS guide. It is not presented as the official Oopbuy website or as an official website of any brand mentioned.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the measurement unit and method, and review chest width, length, waist, rise and inseam as relevant. Letter sizes alone are not enough.

What can QC photos tell me?

Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible details such as color, stitching, shape and measurements. They do not by themselves prove authenticity, durability or a perfect fit.

How do I estimate the total cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the shipping quote, currency and terms before paying.
